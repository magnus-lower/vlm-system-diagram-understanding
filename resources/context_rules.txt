When you look at the system diagram, you have to follow these rules:

- Only include components that are clearly visible in the diagram
- Only include connections that can be clearly traced between components
- Do not assume that two components are connected just because it would make sense
- Do not guess what a component does if the diagram does not explain it
- Do not add components or connections that are not shown
- If something is unclear, do not guess
- Do not identify lines or arrows as components
- Only include the direction of a connection when it is explicitly shown in the diagram
- If several components share the same line, do not automatically assume that they are directly connected to each other
- Do not automatically assume that components on parallel branches are directly connected
