Diagram notation:

- Solid lines show connections between components
- Arrows show the direction of flow
- Parts of the system can be represented by simplified figures
- A larger shape can represent a larger part of the system
- A separate symbol on a line can represent a component even if it has no label
- Labels along connection lines can describe flows or other information and are not necessarily components
- A line can split or join without creating a new component
- A line can form a loop and connect back to the system
- Multiple similar components which are shown together under the same label can be seen as one component group
